I'm Hasan Hüseyin Yurdagül, an ML Engineer and R&D Consultant. I design and ship machine learning systems where wrong predictions have real costs — model development, evaluation, and MLOps, from pilot to production.

I'm currently leading the AI R&D roadmap at Universal Software, mentoring engineers and working on forecasting and support intelligence. Alongside that role, I consult for Moka on fintech models and their serving pipelines.

## Consulting

My work starts with the decision a model should support, then a data audit: what the target means, what information is available at prediction time, and whether the data supports the promise. From there I compare models, communicate the results, and package the useful parts as reports, apps, or APIs.

That has meant connecting call-volume forecasts to Erlang staffing calculations at Ikas, building search and delivery analytics at Inveon, and implementing model-specific feature contracts for batch inference at Moka. Mentoring is part of the handoff too: two Albert Solino engineers built their own Flask version of [MLStudio](@/projects/mlstudio.md), and three Gtech data scientists built a desktop one with my guidance. The [work](@/work.md) page has the roles, engagement dates, and tech stacks; Moka is my only active consultancy.
