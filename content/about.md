I'm Hasan Hüseyin Yurdagül, an ML Engineer and R&D Consultant. I design and ship machine learning systems where wrong predictions have real costs — model development, evaluation, and MLOps, from pilot to production.

I'm currently leading the AI R&D roadmap at Universal Software, and I consult R&D Centers on time-series forecasting, attention networks, and production ML pipelines.

## Consulting

I help teams get models out of notebooks and into operation: scoping the decision the model should change, building the evaluation harness that proves it works, and hardening the serving path so it keeps working.

A typical engagement starts with a short discovery call, followed by a fixed-scope pilot with a written success metric. The full roster of past and active engagements, with dates and tech stacks, is on the [work](/work) page.
