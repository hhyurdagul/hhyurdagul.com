I'm HHYurdagul, a machine learning engineer and consultant. I design and ship ML systems where wrong predictions have real costs — model development, evaluation, and MLOps, from pilot to production.

## Consulting

I help teams get models out of notebooks and into operation: scoping the decision the model should change, building the evaluation harness that proves it works, and hardening the serving path so it keeps working.

A typical engagement starts with a short discovery call, followed by a fixed-scope pilot with a written success metric. If the pilot doesn't beat the baseline by a margin worth operating, we stop — that's a good outcome too.

## Background

- Model development, evaluation, and LLM systems in production.
- MLOps: serving, monitoring, and cost control for inference workloads.
- I write up what I learn in the [blog](@/blog/_index.md).
