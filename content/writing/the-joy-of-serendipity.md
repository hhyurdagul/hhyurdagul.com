I was originally thinking of something other than serendipity—well, actually,
not other than. I was thinking of the joy of note-taking, and also about the game
and the music I came across on the incremental games page of itch.io. The game
is very pleasant; it has a great, relaxing soundtrack, which I will link at the
end, but I can't stop writing now to do it immediately. I feel like I was born
to write but just couldn't find the time to do it properly.

What the game is about is not necessarily important, but what it led me to *is*.
While playing, I found the soundtrack was so pleasant that I both wanted to put
on my headset to feel it in my ears as close as possible, without distraction
from the outer world, and wanted to make my own. I believe the music itself was
created with a digital piano. I am not a music expert, but I know this is some
sort of piano-like instrument while sounding a bit digital. So this led me to
think about whether I could manage to do the same, not that I know how to play
an instrument, but I know I am great with melodies and rhythms, which made me
believe I can follow them to create decent music. The one thing that scares me
about this process is whether it will be a chaotic one rather than a simple
one. I just want to put blocks in place, or click the piano keys at a given
time, and save that to produce the music I want.

When I look at it, I believe the reason this led me to write the title with the
word "Serendipity" is actually how spontaneous all of these processes are. I
just wanted to play a game, which led me to want to listen to the music, which
led me to want to write music, while wanting to note all of these thoughts down,
which made me smile because—as it seems—I really love writing. All of this
enlightenment has made me discover serendipity through serendipity. The joy of
serendipity it is.
